Community

Community Rules

Effective 9 September 2026

Be specific, truthful, constructive, and respectful.

Allowed participation

  • Describe a real problem, intended audience, and possible solution.
  • Discuss needs and works constructively.
  • Submit only a dApp already listed in the Solana dApp Store and only when you have permission to represent it.
  • Use promotion to buy labeled visibility, never votes or natural rank.

Prohibited content

  • Scams, impersonation, phishing, malicious software, or deceptive links.
  • Sexual exploitation, pornography, hate, threats, harassment, criminal instruction, or illegal trade.
  • Political campaigning, propaganda, fundraising, election manipulation, political violence, and political topics restricted by this community, including Chinese political leaders, the Communist Party, separatist movements, and sensitive political events. Ordinary place names and nonpolitical public-service product needs are allowed.
  • False profit claims, guaranteed returns, manipulated engagement, spam, or undisclosed paid endorsements.
  • Copyright violations, personal data shared without authority, or content that violates Solana dApp Store rules.

Automated screening

Public text is checked by server-side rules and Cloudflare Workers AI before publication. If review is unavailable, publication is paused and you can retry. Previously published text matching current rules is hidden from public feeds, search and details; the original remains available to the owner and moderators until explicitly deleted. High-confidence violations are rejected. Rejected text and matched phrases are not retained; only the account, publishing surface, policy category, and time are recorded for abuse prevention. Screening can make mistakes, so reports and appeals remain available.

Enforcement

Users can report content. LionDApp may reject or remove content, stop a recommendation, preserve limited evidence, and block the associated .skr identity from community actions. A blocked user may still browse. Appeals are available through support.

社区

社区规则

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 9 日

请具体、真实、建设性并尊重他人。

允许的参与

  • 描述真实问题、适用人群和可能的解决方式。
  • 建设性讨论需求与作品。
  • 只提交已经在 Solana dApp Store 上架且你有权代表的 dApp。
  • 推广只能购买明确标注的曝光，不能购买点赞或自然排名。

禁止内容

  • 诈骗、仿冒、钓鱼、恶意软件或欺骗性链接。
  • 性剥削、色情、仇恨、威胁、骚扰、犯罪教程或违法交易。
  • 党派竞选、政治宣传、募款、操纵选举、政治暴力，以及本社区限制的中国政治领导人、共产党、分裂主义和敏感政治事件等话题。普通地名和不涉及政治的公共服务产品需求可以讨论。
  • 虚假收益、保证回报、操纵互动、垃圾信息或未披露的付费宣传。
  • 侵权、无权公开的个人数据或违反 Solana dApp Store 规则的内容。

自动筛查

公开文字在发布前会经过服务端规则和 Cloudflare Workers AI 检查；审核不可用时暂停发布，可稍后重试。历史内容命中当前规则后停止在公开列表、搜索和详情展示，原始记录保留给作者和后台，直到主动删除。高置信度违规内容会被拒绝。被拒绝的正文和命中短语不会保留，仅为防止滥用记录账号、发布入口、规则类别和时间。筛查可能误判，因此仍保留举报和申诉途径。

执行

用户可以举报内容。LionDApp 可以拒绝或下架内容、停止推荐、有限保留证据，并禁止关联 .skr 身份继续参与社区。被封禁用户仍可浏览，并可通过支持渠道申诉。
