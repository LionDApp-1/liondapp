HOW IT WORKS测试流程

One clear path, from invitation to result.从邀请到成果，路径清楚。

A testing report is reviewed against published requirements. It is not a paid positive review.测试成果按事先公开的要求验收。奖励不以好评为条件。

  1. 01

    Create an invitation发起邀请

    Choose a Store dApp, define the steps, capacity and acceptance criteria.选择商店 dApp，明确步骤、人数及验收标准。

  2. 02

    Reserve a spot报名测试

    Read the requirements and your submission deadline before joining.先查看要求和自己的提交时限，再报名。

  3. 03

    Test & submit体验并提交

    Describe what you tried, what happened and what could improve.说明测试内容、实际结果和改进建议。

  4. 04

    Review & follow up验收与跟进

    Hosts review within 72 hours. Corrections and platform review have their own states.发起者在 72 小时内验收，补充成果与平台复核有明确状态。

REWARD MODEL悬赏规则

Your reward.
A transparent fee.奖励到手，
费用透明。

The intended model: creators predeposit all tester rewards plus a 10% platform fee. Testers receive the stated reward in full after approval.目标规则：发起者预存全部测试奖励，另加 10% 平台费；测试者验收通过后获得标明的完整奖励。

Unused rewards and their fees are refundable after recruitment closes and all obligations are resolved. Fees round up per tester to the smallest SKR unit.停止招募且所有成果与申诉结案后，未使用奖励及对应手续费可退回。每人手续费向上取整至 SKR 最小单位。

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW常见问题

Clear before you join.参与之前，了解清楚。

Can I browse without a wallet?不连接钱包可以浏览吗？+

Yes. Discovery is public. Posting, following, messaging and testing require wallet authentication and a valid .skr domain.可以。应用发现公开开放；发帖、关注、私信及报名测试需要钱包登录并验证有效的 .skr 域名。

Do builders have to be verified developers?发起者都是已认证的开发者吗？+

No. Linking an app identifies the subject of a post. It does not certify developer affiliation. Read the requirements and public history before participating.不是。关联应用仅用于标明讨论对象，不证明开发者身份。参与前请查看活动要求和公开历史。

Does criticism qualify as a testing result?负面反馈能通过验收吗？+

Yes, if the result meets the published test requirements. Rewards must not depend on praise. A rejected result can be appealed within 72 hours; overdue reviews can be sent for platform review.能，只要符合公开测试要求。奖励不得以好评为条件；被拒绝的成果可在 72 小时内申诉，超时未验收的成果也可申请平台复核。

Can I earn real SKR right now?现在能获得真实 SKR 吗？+

Real campaign funding and payouts are disabled. Free invitations and reward drafts are supported in the current build. Simulated receipts are test data and are not token transfers.当前真实悬赏预存和结算关闭。构建支持免费邀请与悬赏草稿。模拟凭证仅为测试数据，不是代币转账。

Which languages are supported?支持哪些语言？+

English is the default, with a Chinese interface to make dApps easier to use for Chinese-speaking Seeker users. User posts remain in their original language. More languages will depend on feedback.默认英文，同时提供中文界面，帮助英语不熟练的中文区 Seeker 用户。用户帖子保留原语言，后续语言支持根据反馈决定。

Is this an official Solana product?这是 Solana 官方产品吗？+

LionDApp is an independent project. Store metadata is sourced from the official catalog. Official recommendation, Store approval or activity rewards have not been confirmed.LionDApp 是独立项目，商店应用信息来自官方目录。官方推荐、商店审核通过或活动奖励均未确认。
