Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026

Data we process

We process your public wallet address, resolved .skr domain, signed login message and signature, short-lived session data, language, optional profile text and link, posts, comments, reactions, reports, uploaded images, recommendation orders, public transaction signatures, in-app notifications, and security logs. We never ask for or store seed phrases or private keys.

Purpose

We use the data to authenticate .skr users, operate community features, review works, prevent abuse, deliver notifications, confirm recommendation payments, respond to support, and meet legal obligations. We use aggregate operational counts and technical server logs to maintain the service. This version does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not use advertising trackers or create cross-app profiles.

Public data and blockchains

Your .skr domain and submitted community content are public. A .skr domain can be resolved to a public wallet address. Confirmed Solana transactions are public and cannot be deleted or changed by LionDApp.

Providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1, R2, security, administration access and automated text moderation. Submitted posts, profiles, comments and private message text are sent to Workers AI for safety classification; account identifiers and wallet signatures are not included. Public app-store descriptions are also sent to Workers AI to generate Chinese translations. Notifications are currently shown inside the app; Firebase push and crash diagnostics are not integrated. Solana RPC and AllDomains provide public-chain and .skr resolution. Data may be processed in multiple countries.

Retention and deletion

Published needs, works, comments and their images have no automatic expiry while the service operates. Hiding a policy violation does not itself delete its record. Account deletion immediately ends sessions and removes public display. Ordinary content and image backups are cleared within 30 days. security and moderation records are kept for 180 days. Recommendation orders and blockchain evidence are kept for two years. Public blockchain data remains on-chain.

Private messages

Project conversations are accessible to their two participants through the app. Messages are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Text is checked by Workers AI before delivery. Administrators can review reported messages for abuse; we do not provide an operator interface for browsing unreported conversations. Unremoved messages have no automatic expiry. Withdrawing a message hides it from both participants immediately; ordinary removed message text is cleared after 30 days. Evidence attached to an open abuse report may be retained until the report is resolved and subsequently cleared by the retention job.

Voluntary tips

When tipping is enabled, we record the prepared transaction, public wallet address, amount and confirmation signature. The wallet signs and sends the transfer directly to the project wallet. We never handle payments between users.

Your choices

You can delete content and request account deletion in the app. Contact support for access, correction of optional profile data, objection, or appeal where applicable. Because published content cannot be edited, changed content must be deleted and reposted.

隐私

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 17 日

处理的数据

我们处理公开钱包地址、解析出的 .skr 域名、登录签名消息和签名、短期会话数据、语言、可选简介和链接、帖子、评论、互动、举报、上传图片、推荐订单、公开交易签名、应用内通知及安全日志。我们绝不会索取或存储助记词和私钥。

用途

数据用于验证 .skr 用户、运行社区功能、审核作品、防止滥用、发送通知、确认推荐付款、提供支持和履行法律义务。我们使用运营汇总数量和服务端技术日志维护服务；本版本没有接入第三方统计或崩溃报告 SDK，不使用广告追踪器或建立跨应用画像。

公开数据与区块链

你的 .skr 域名和提交的社区内容是公开的。.skr 可解析到公开钱包地址。已确认的 Solana 交易公开存在，LionDApp 无法删除或修改。

服务商与跨境处理

Cloudflare 提供托管、D1、R2、安全、后台访问和自动文字审核；待发布的帖子、资料、评论与私信文字会交给 Workers AI 判断内容安全，不附带账户标识和钱包签名。公开商店应用介绍也会交给 Workers AI 生成中文译文。当前通知在应用内显示，未接入 Firebase 推送或崩溃诊断；Solana RPC 与 AllDomains 提供公链和 .skr 解析。数据可能在多个国家处理。

保留与删除

服务运营期间，已发布需求、作品、评论及其图片不会因发布时间而自动到期删除。违规屏蔽本身不会删除原始记录。删除账户后立即结束会话并停止公开展示。普通内容和图片备份在 30 天内清除，安全与审核记录保留 180 天，推荐订单和链上凭证保留 2 年，公开链上数据继续存在。

私信

应用内项目对话仅向双方参与者开放。消息存储在服务端，不是端到端加密；发送前由 Workers AI 审核。管理员可查看被举报的消息以处理滥用，后台不提供浏览未举报对话的入口。未删除消息没有自动到期时间。撤回立即对双方隐藏正文，普通已撤回消息在 30 天后清除正文；关联未结案举报的证据可能保留至举报处理完成，再由保留任务清理。

自愿打赏

打赏开放后，我们记录待签交易、公开钱包地址、金额和确认签名。转账由用户钱包签名并直接发送到项目钱包，平台不处理用户之间的付款。

你的选择

你可以在应用内删除内容和申请删除账户。适用时可联系支持请求访问、更正可选资料、反对处理或申诉。已发布内容不能编辑，需要删除后重新发布。
