Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Data we process

We process your public wallet address, resolved .skr domain, signed login message and signature, short-lived sessions, language, optional profile text and link, posts, app associations, feedback types, responses, author-reported progress and outcomes, content revisions, follow preferences, willingness to test, reactions, reports, uploaded images, recommendation orders, public transaction signatures, in-app notifications and security logs. We never ask for or store seed phrases or private keys.

Purpose

We use data to authenticate users, operate discussions and app discovery, deliver updates about followed apps and questions, review content, prevent abuse, respond to support and meet legal obligations. Aggregate operational counts and technical logs maintain the service. There is no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK, advertising tracker or cross-app profile.

Public content and private preferences

Your .skr domain, posts, app feedback, responses and reported results are public. Individual follow and test preferences are visible only to their account through the app; public questions show aggregate follower and volunteer counts, not participant lists. Content revisions are retained for integrity and have no public browsing interface. A .skr domain can be resolved to a public wallet address. Confirmed blockchain transactions cannot be deleted by LionDApp. Community feedback is not written to official Store reviews or ratings.

Providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1, R2, security, administration access and automated text moderation. Submitted posts, edits, profiles, responses and private messages are sent to Workers AI for safety classification, without account identifiers or wallet signatures. Public Store descriptions are also sent to Workers AI for Chinese translation. Solana RPC and AllDomains provide public-chain and .skr resolution. Processing may occur in multiple countries. Notifications are in-app; Firebase push and crash diagnostics are not integrated.

Retention and deletion

Active published content and images have no automatic expiry. Edits retain previous versions while the post remains active. Hiding a violation does not delete its record. Account deletion ends sessions, removes public display and clears follow preferences. Ordinary removed content, its revisions and images are cleared after 30 days by retention jobs; private snapshots follow the backup retention policy. Security, moderation and notification records are retained for 180 days. Recommendation orders and blockchain evidence are retained for two years. Public blockchain data remains on-chain.

Private messages

Conversations are accessible to their two participants through the app. Messages are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Workers AI checks text before delivery. Administrators can review reported messages; there is no operator interface for browsing unreported conversations. Unremoved messages have no automatic expiry. Withdrawal immediately hides text from both participants. Ordinary withdrawn text is cleared after 30 days; open-report evidence may be retained until resolution and subsequent cleanup.

Testing data and financial gates

Campaign terms, reservations, submission deadlines, reports, evidence links, review/correction reasons, appeals, resolutions and event history are stored in D1. A tester can access their own entry; the host can access reports in their campaign; authorized operators can access disputed or overdue reports. Reports are not automatically public comments. Submitted text is screened with Workers AI. A link may lead to a third-party service with its own privacy policy.

Campaign and participant records have no automatic expiry in the current implementation. Account deletion is restricted until testing obligations are resolved; contact support for data requests. This does not remove public blockchain evidence. Real campaign payments are currently disabled; simulated funding and settlement records do not represent transfers.

Voluntary tips

If enabled, tipping records the prepared transaction, public wallet address, amount and confirmation signature. Your wallet signs and sends directly to the project wallet. Tips are separate from testing rewards and are currently disabled.

Your choices

You can edit questions and feedback after another moderation check, delete content and accounts, stop following apps or questions, and change your test preference. Comments cannot be edited. Contact support for access, correction, objection or appeal where applicable.

隐私

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

处理的数据

我们处理公开钱包地址、解析出的 .skr 域名、登录签名消息和签名、短期会话、语言、可选简介及链接、帖子、应用关联、评价类型、回应、作者报告的进展与结果、内容旧版本、关注偏好、测试意愿、互动、举报、上传图片、推荐订单、公开交易签名、应用内消息和安全日志。绝不会索取或存储助记词和私钥。

用途

数据用于验证用户、运行讨论与应用发现、通知所关注应用及问题的更新、审核内容、防止滥用、提供支持和履行法律义务。运营汇总数量和技术日志用于维护服务；没有第三方统计或崩溃报告 SDK、广告追踪器或跨应用画像。

公开内容与私人偏好

你的 .skr、帖子、应用评价、回应和使用结果公开展示。应用内的个人关注和测试偏好仅向本人开放；公开问题只展示汇总关注和测试人数，不提供参与者名单。旧版本为内容完整性保留，没有公开浏览入口。.skr 可解析到公开钱包地址，已确认链上交易无法由平台删除。社区评价不回写官方商店评价或评分。

服务商与跨境处理

Cloudflare 提供托管、D1、R2、安全、后台访问和自动文字审核。提交的帖子、修改、资料、回应及私信由 Workers AI 判断安全，不附带账户标识或钱包签名。公开商店介绍也会交给 Workers AI 生成中文译文。Solana RPC 和 AllDomains 提供公链及 .skr 解析，数据可能在多个国家处理。当前只有应用内消息，未接入 Firebase 推送或崩溃诊断。

保留与删除

有效公开内容及图片没有自动到期时间。帖子有效期间保留修改前版本。违规屏蔽本身不删除记录。删除账户后结束会话、停止公开展示并清除关注偏好。普通已删除内容、旧版本及图片由保留任务在 30 天后清理；私有快照遵循备份保留政策。安全、审核与消息记录保留 180 天，推荐订单及链上凭证保留两年。公开链上数据继续存在。

私信

应用内对话仅向双方参与者开放，消息存储在服务端，不是端到端加密。Workers AI 在发送前审核。管理员可查看被举报消息，后台不提供浏览未举报对话的入口。未删除消息没有自动到期时间，撤回立即对双方隐藏文字；普通撤回正文在 30 天后清理，未结案举报证据可保留至处理完成及后续清理。

测试数据与付款开关

活动条件、报名、提交期限、测试成果、证明链接、验收与补充理由、申诉、复核及事件历史保存在 D1。测试者可访问自己的报名记录；发起者可访问本活动成果；授权运营人员可访问争议及超时成果。测试成果不会自动成为公开评论。提交文字由 Workers AI 筛查；证明链接可能指向适用其自身隐私政策的第三方服务。

当前实现未对活动和参与记录设置自动到期。测试义务结案前会限制账户删除；数据请求可联系支持。公开区块链凭证无法删除。真实悬赏付款尚未开放，模拟预存及结算记录不代表转账。

自愿打赏

打赏开放时记录待签交易、公开钱包地址、金额和确认签名。用户钱包签名并直接发送至项目钱包，打赏与测试悬赏分开，当前打赏关闭。

你的选择

问题和评价可重新审核后编辑，也可删除内容及账户、取消应用和问题关注、修改测试意愿。评论不能编辑。适用时可联系支持请求访问、更正、反对处理或申诉。
