# Product scope

## Literal outcome

A Seeker owner can search for a product that solves a problem, publish the unmet need when no result exists, and discover developer-submitted dApps that are already available in the Solana dApp Store.

## In scope

- Anonymous browsing and keyword search.
- Structured needs plus a reduced-field `Wild Ideas / 天马行空` collection for open-ended concepts.
- `.skr`-gated needs, works, comments, replies, reactions, reports, and recommendation purchases.
- Needs and works as separate main surfaces; search on Needs returns both.
- Work review before publication.
- Seven-day, clearly labeled, dynamically priced SKR recommendations.
- Personal content history, notifications, content deletion, and account deletion.
- Cloudflare Access-protected operator console.
- Chinese and English UI/legal content, with English as the first-launch default and an in-app persistent language switch; user content remains untranslated.
- Imported Solana dApp Store results may have Chinese presentation text generated and cached by LionDApp. This translation exists only inside LionDApp search and detail views; it never edits or republishes the official Store's metadata.

## Explicit non-goals

- Matching, claims, development status, milestones, delivery verification, escrow, bounties, or private messaging.
- APK hosting or direct downloads.
- Star ratings or separate review scores.
- KYC, legal-name collection, or an additional public nickname.
- A web community client.
- Platform token issuance or DAO governance.

## Success signals

- Search-to-existing-work click-through.
- Needs receiving “I need this” reactions and substantive comments.
- Approved works receiving detail views and reactions.
- Return rate of `.skr` identities.
- Report response time and confirmed recommendation uptime.
