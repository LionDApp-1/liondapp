# Hackathon submission draft — LionDApp

Updated 7 October 2026. Adjust to the actual event form and accepted artifact. No official recommendation or Store approval has been confirmed.

**One line:** LionDApp connects Seeker dApp discovery with real feedback and structured community testing.

**Problem:** Downloads and generic comments do not explain where users encounter friction. Seeker users need a place to discuss the specific dApps they use; builders need clear, reviewable feedback from testers.

**Solution:** An anonymous Store catalog leads into app-specific issues, suggestions and praise. Builders/users can recommend a listed app and publish testing steps, capacity, deadlines and report requirements. Testers reserve a place and submit results independently of public comments; hosts review against published criteria, with correction, appeal and platform-review states. English defaults; Chinese broadens accessibility for users who find English difficult.

**What is built:** Native Kotlin/Compose Android, MWA/SIWS and .skr authentication; Cloudflare Worker/D1/R2, rules + Workers AI moderation and catalog translation; bilingual operator console and responsive website with real catalog search and an exact-unit fee estimator. Free invitations, private reward drafts and off-chain review workflows are implemented and tested.

**Business model:** Planned creator-funded SKR testing: net tester reward plus a separate 10% platform fee, with full predeposit and unused-balance refund. Real funds are disabled. The Anchor escrow has a compiled SBF artifact and 13 passing isolated account/CPI tests; deployment and confirmed transaction/MWA integration remain pending. Simulation is explicitly isolated and not revenue or paid-user evidence.

**Evidence:** Release 1.2.1 / code 4 is signed with the original certificate and submitted to the Store, In Review. The public snapshot passes 122 Node tests, 8 Python tests, API/site type checks, Android debug build, six JVM tests and lint. The free lifecycle was checked with two accounts on physical Seeker on 1.2.0, followed by upgrade/history checks on 1.2.1. Different-wallet switching remains untested. See [release metadata](../submission/release-1.2.1.json) and [source checks](../submission/source-verification.json).

**Validation plan:** Recruit real dApp builders and Seeker testers, measure substantive report completion, review time, disputes and return use after incentives end. Store featuring and seasonal rewards may help distribution, but are not a substitute for demand evidence or a confirmed partnership. X engagement tasks remain out of this first phase.
